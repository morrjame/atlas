Atlas privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

  1. Atlas is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not offered to other users.
  2. Atlas accesses the owner's own Google account data (Drive, Sheets, Tasks, BigQuery) and the owner's Todoist account, only to show, sync and update the owner's own records.
  3. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.
  4. Data stays in the owner's Google account and Todoist account. Atlas keeps no separate copy outside them, except a temporary copy in the owner's own browser for offline use.
  5. Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings.
